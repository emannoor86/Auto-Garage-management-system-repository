Information Objectives:

The system will collect and maintain information related to customers, 
vehicles, service requests, repair tasks, employees, attendance, spare parts, 
billing, and payments. It will record customer and vehicle details, service and 
repair information, mechanic assignments, employee check-in and check-out timings, 
inventory updates, and payment details.

The system will provide organized information such as customer and vehicle records, 
service history, repair status, assigned tasks, inventory records, invoices, 
payment status, employee attendance and working hours, salary information, and 
business reports. It will also provide summaries of vehicles by make and body type, 
service records, financial information, and dashboard summaries for authorized users.
