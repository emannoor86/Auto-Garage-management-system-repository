Constraints 
The system will only handle the management and recording of auto garage activities. The system will not do the repair or maintenance of vehicles. 
Access to system functions depends on the role and permissions assigned to each user. The accuracy of reports and calculations depends on how correct the data entered by userss.
The system needs a computer, a database and a network to work properly. The system also relies on authorized users to update customer, vehicle, employee, inventory and payment information.
