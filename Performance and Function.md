**3. Function and Performance**
   **3.1 Function :**
   The auto garage management system is for managing main activities in an auto garage . The system will allow authorized
    users to record and manage customer and vehicle information, maintain service records, manage appointments, and keep track of the
    services provided to vehicles.This system will organize records given by user . It facilitates user to access data easily to avoid
   data duplication etc. The system will also provide billing information and will calculate and show the loss , expenses etc. on
   dashboard .
   **3.2 Performance :**
   The system will provide quick and satisfactory response to its users . Customer , vehicle and service record will
   be manages efficiently .The system should be able to handle the expected number of customers, vehicles, service records, and users
    of the garage The system should also respond within a short amount of time when users add, search, update, or
    retrieve information.
